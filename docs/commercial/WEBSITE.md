# Keep an eye on your laptop, even when you're away.

LaptopWatch is being built to turn your own laptop camera and Android phone into a security companion: spot activity, review a live view, and remotely lock your workstation with a tap.

### Intended experience

1. Install the LaptopWatch companion on your Windows, macOS or Linux laptop.
2. Pair an Android phone by scanning a QR code and approving the device.
3. Arm monitoring and receive person-detection alerts; stop recording whenever you choose.

### Planned capabilities

- Private encrypted live video and two-way audio.
- Direct local-network connections, with opt-in remote access.
- On-device person detection to cut down false alarms.
- OS-level remote lock, audible deterrence and searchable local events.
- Clear camera/microphone indicators, easy unpairing and no covert mode.

### Important limits

LaptopWatch helps deter theft but cannot prevent physical theft or guarantee detection. Network access, camera availability and background restrictions vary by device. Monitoring and recording must comply with applicable laws and consent requirements.
